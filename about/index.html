From our family table to yours.

Fanmi Tab. The family table. Our name reflects the heart of Fanmi Tab: food, family, and gathering. At our table, culinary artistry meets the warmth of being welcomed home.

The chef

Chef Kyle Martin

Fanmi Tab is the catering company of Chef Kyle Martin. Kyle cooks the way he was raised to — with intention, generosity, and fine-dining standards carried into the room where people actually live: your home.

Every Fanmi Tab experience is created, directed, and overseen by Chef Kyle personally — from the first menu conversation through the final plate. Alongside his own tables, he teaches as a chef and barista instructor in Houston, which keeps his craft in daily practice and his standards high.

Rooted in thoughtful preparation, quality ingredients, and an appreciation for the experience of dining, his approach brings fine-dining standards and personal care to every table Fanmi Tab serves.

The coordinator

Shantā Martin — your point of contact

A Fanmi Tab engagement runs on good coordination as much as good cooking. Shantā, Kyle's wife, coordinates Fanmi Tab's events — she is the person who will answer your inquiry, confirm the details, and make sure nothing about your table is left to chance.

When you write to Fanmi Tab, you are writing to family — and your event is planned like it matters to ours.

Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

We respect the personal information you share with Fanmi Tab. This notice explains how our website and inquiry process handle that information.

Contact

For questions about privacy or your inquiry, email fanmitab@gmail.com.

Information you share

We use the information you choose to provide to review your request, respond to you, and discuss the culinary services or experience you have in mind.

Our inquiry form is provided by Tally. Our inquiry buttons open our dedicated Google Sites inquiry page in a new tab, where the Tally form is provided. Information submitted through the form is processed through Tally. You may contact us by email instead.

Please share only what is needed for your inquiry. Do not send payment credentials, identification documents, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Website and external services

Fanmi Tab’s custom website code includes no advertising pixels or analytics and does not store visitor preferences. Its images are included in this preview; the lightweight version uses a browser font. The hosting provider receives the technical information needed to deliver the page.

External services handle information under their own policies. Read Tally’s privacy policy ↗︎. When this website is displayed in Google Sites, Google also operates the surrounding page; see Google’s privacy policy ↗︎. Following a social link takes you to that platform.

Questions and requests

Contact fanmitab@gmail.com with questions about how your inquiry information is used or retained, or to request access, correction, or deletion. The rights available to you depend on applicable law and your circumstances.

Cookie Policy

Cookie Policy
