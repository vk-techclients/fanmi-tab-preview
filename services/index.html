Services

Curated around your table.

Every Fanmi Tab service begins the same way — with your table. Your guests, your occasion, your setting. Menus are created around them; there is no fixed menu, by design, and every engagement is quoted personally after a short inquiry.

I.

In-Home Weekly Chef Service

Personalized, recurring chef services bringing thoughtful menu curation, culinary expertise, and freshly prepared meals into your home. From menu curation and ingredient sourcing to in-home preparation and cleanup, Fanmi Tab creates meals around your household, preferences, and the way you live.

Recurring weekly and biweekly arrangements in Houston. In-home chef service, not meal delivery. Service details are confirmed personally.

How it starts: 01 · Tell us about your table — preferences, dietary considerations, ideal schedule. 02 · Make room for a rhythm — a recurring arrangement and menus that feel right. 03 · Let your kitchen come to life — your chef cooks in your kitchen.

II.

The Fanmi Tab Experience — Intimate & Private Dining

A chef-led fine-dining experience, personally curated and prepared around your guests, occasion, and setting. Every experience is approached with intention, from the first stages of preparation through the final plate — original culinary experiences bringing together food, artistry, and thoughtfully curated entertainment.

III.

Retreats & Gatherings

Thoughtfully directed culinary services for private gatherings, retreats, and engagements requiring multiple meals or extended service — one chef-led thread running through your whole event, so the table never feels like an afterthought.

IV.

Corporate & Organizational Engagements

Professional culinary services for businesses, organizations, productions, and corporate occasions — planned with the same care as a family table, run with the reliability a production schedule demands.

V.

Custom Culinary Engagements

Collaborative culinary experiences developed around distinctive concepts, occasions, and creative opportunities. If it gathers people around food, it belongs at the Fanmi Tab table — tell us the idea and we'll shape it together.
